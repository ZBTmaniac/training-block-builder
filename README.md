# Training Block Builder

Build a run training plan you can keep on your phone, offline, with no account.

- Answer a few questions (goal race, experience, recent volume, days per week)
  and get a plan built on published coaching principles.
- Or upload your own plan from the spreadsheet template.
- Add strength training matched to the equipment you have.
- Export to PDF, Excel, or your calendar, or save it to your home screen.

Flags, not rules: the tool tells you when a goal or timeline looks like a stretch,
then builds the plan you choose.

Not medical advice. Listen to your body.
